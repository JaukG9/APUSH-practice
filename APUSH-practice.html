How to Play & Powerups

The Timer: You have 15 seconds per question. The faster you lock in a correct answer, the more points you get (60 to 100 pts). Wrong answers give 0.

The Shop: Between rounds, you have 5 seconds to buy powerups using your score points!

  • Mr. Smith (Cost: 50): Eliminates 2 wrong answers (50/50) on the next question.
  • Mr. Wendt (Cost: 75): Eliminates ALL wrong answers (Auto-corrects).
  • Manifest Destiny (Cost: 80): Earn double points if you get the next question right.
  • Monroe Doctrine (Cost: 100): Nullifies the very next attack used against you (stays active until hit).
  • Trustbusting (Cost: 150): Destroys 200 points from the current 1st Place player (the richest).
  • Spoils System (Cost: 150): Steals 100 points from a random opponent.
  • Embargo Act (Cost: 200): Forces a random opponent to earn 0 points on the next question.
  • Gilded Age (Cost: 50): 50% chance to instantly gain 150 pts, 50% chance to lose 100 pts.
