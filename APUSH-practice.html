How to Play & Powerups

The Timer: You have 7 seconds per question. Faster correct answers grant more points (60 to 100). Wrong answers yield 0.

The Shop: Between rounds, you have 5 seconds to buy powerups using your score points!

  • Mr. Smith (Cost: 50): Eliminates 2 wrong answers (50/50).
  • Mr. Wendt (Cost: 150): Eliminates ALL wrong answers (Auto-corrects).
  • Manifest Destiny (Cost: 50): Earn double points if correct.
  • Monroe Doctrine (Cost: 100): Nullifies the very next attack against you.
  • Trustbusting (Cost: 150): Destroys 200 points from the current 1st Place player.
  • Spoils System (Cost: 150): Steals 100 points from a random opponent.
  • Embargo Act (Cost: 200): Forces an opponent to earn 0 points next question.
  • Gilded Age (Cost: 50): 50% chance to gain 150 pts, 50% chance to lose 100 pts.
