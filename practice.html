How to Play & Powerups

The Timer: If enabled, faster correct answers grant more points. Wrong answers yield 0. If disabled, correct answers give a flat 100 points.

The Shop (Multiplayer Only): Between rounds, you have 5 seconds to buy powerups using your score points!

  • Abraham Lincoln (Cost: 50): Eliminates 2 wrong answers (50/50).
  • George Washington (Cost: 150): Eliminates ALL wrong answers (Auto-corrects).
  • Manifest Destiny (Cost: 50): Earn double points if correct.
  • Monroe Doctrine (Cost: 100): Nullifies the very next attack against you (Stacks!).
  • Trustbusting (Cost: 150): Destroys up to 200 points from the 1st Place player.
  • Spoils System (Cost: 150): Steals up to 100 points from ONE random opponent.
  • Embargo Act (Cost: 200): Forces an opponent to earn 0 points next question.
  • Gilded Age (Cost: 75): 50% chance to gain 200 pts, 50% chance to lose 100 pts.
  • Limit: You can only use 3 powerups per round.
